Creator kit: recording, editing and voice

From screen recording to music: tools for each step, and what to check before commercial use.

Updated 19 October 2026 · 3 min read

Recording

OBS Studio records video and streams live, for example to Twitch and YouTube. It is free and open source. For audio only, Audacity records, edits and converts formats, also for free.

Editing

Descript lets you edit video and audio through the transcript, with AI tools for removing filler words and adding captions. DaVinci Resolve is a professional editor with a free version.

Voice and music

ElevenLabs generates voice from text. Suno creates music, but note: according to its pricing page, the free plan has no commercial use rights. Only the Pro and Premier plans include them.

Commercial use

Before you publish content made with an AI tool, check the license terms for your plan on the official page.
