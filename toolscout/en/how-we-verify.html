How we verify tools

On every ToolScout page you can see what was checked, when, against which source, and what hasn't been verified yet.

63 tools · 61 with verified pricing · 2 with unverified pricing · 5 books

Updated 19 October 2026

  1. 1. Official sources only

    We check each tool's pricing model (free, free + paid, or paid only) and plan names on its official pricing page. Not blogs, not round-ups, not AI models.

  2. 2. Every fact has a source

    Each pro and con shown as a "verified fact" links to the page it came from: the pricing page or the official site.

  3. 3. What isn't verified is marked

    If an official page doesn't state something, such as pricing or platforms, we mark it "Not verified" instead of filling it in from memory.

  4. 4. Every check is dated

    Each tool shows the date it was checked. Plans and prices change, so we re-check regularly.

What we don't publish

  • Ratings, scores or stars: we don't have a real data source for them.
  • Reviews or testimonials in users' names.
  • Exact prices without a source and date. Instead we show the pricing model, with a link to the official page.
  • Popularity data. "Editors' picks" is an editorial selection, and is labeled as such.

Facts vs editorial summary

Each tool's description, audience and uses are ToolScout's summary, and they're clearly labeled "Editorial summary" on the tool page. That way you can tell what we checked from what we summarize.

Money and independence

We currently receive no payment from any tool. If affiliate links or sponsored content are added later, they'll be labeled right next to the link. They won't change verified facts or editors' picks. Disclosure page
